Privacy Policy

This sales site collects as little as possible. Here is exactly what happens with your data.

  1. No accounts, no analytics. We do not ask you to sign up and we run no tracking scripts on these pages.
  2. Your API keys stay yours. Groq keys you use in the projects live on your own computer. They are never sent to us — there is nowhere to send them to.
  3. Hosting logs. Our host (Cloudflare) may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, to operate and protect the site.
  4. Contact. If you email us once an inbox exists, we use your message only to reply.
