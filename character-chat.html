What is AI Character Chat?

AI Character Chat is a free AI roleplay and character chat generator hosted on Perchance. You open a page, pick a character such as Chloe, or create your own, and start a conversation. There is no sign-up screen, no subscription gate and no fixed message limit.

Everything runs in the browser. Characters, lore, messages and memories are saved locally in IndexedDB, so your chats are private by default and remain available offline after the page loads. An optional paid add-on called Keepsake adds cloud backup, restore points and a memory vault for people who want their chats to survive a cleared browser or a new device.

Long-term memory, without losing your chats

The free generator already creates memories and rolling summaries inside each conversation. That is what helps a character remember a promise, a name or an event from earlier in the same chat, even after older messages scroll out of the prompt.

The optional Keepsake plan adds two things on top. First, automatic cloud backup every five minutes, with restore points you can roll back to within the last two hours. Second, a memory vault: every memory your characters have saved is collected into one searchable place across all your chats, so you can look up a detail without reopening every conversation.

  • Keepsake costs ₹129/month and is available in India.
  • Your chats stay saved after the plan ends — nothing is deleted.
  • Backup is opt-in. The free experience stays local and account-free.
